Connection to ACM CTF 3.0

The workshops hosted by the ACM training division are explicitly designed to build foundational skills for the four main challenge categories of the upcoming CTF 3.0: Cryptography, Web Exploitation, Forensics, and OSINT.

Please note: Workshop materials, examples, and practice exercises are for educational preparation only. They will not reveal actual competition challenges, infrastructure, or flags.
